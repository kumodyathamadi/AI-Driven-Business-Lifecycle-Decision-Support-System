# Component 4 — Western Province Business Risk Dataset Package

## Scope
The package is scoped to the Western Province districts Colombo, Gampaha and Kalutara.

## Important provenance statement
The business, financial, relationship, growth-action and outcome rows generated in this package are **simulated scenario data**, not observed records from real Sri Lankan businesses. Neutral filenames are used for integration convenience, but provenance is explicitly marked in the data fields (`data_origin`, `label_source`) and manifest. Do not present these rows as real businesses or as verified historical risk outcomes.

## Files
- `business_profiles.csv`: one row per simulated business.
- `financial_snapshots.csv`: 12 monthly snapshots per business.
- `business_relationships.csv`: simulated supplier, customer and lender edges.
- `growth_actions.csv`: growth-action baseline and mitigation variants.
- `risk_outcomes.csv`: rule-derived risk/readiness labels and explanations.
- `graph_entities.csv`: graph node registry.
- `scenario_families_and_splits.csv`: scenario controls and leakage-safe split assignment.
- `dataset_manifest.json`: schema, seed, rules and limitations.

## Dataset size
- 500 base business scenarios.
- 6,000 monthly financial snapshots.
- 1,000 growth actions, each with baseline and mitigated variants (2,000 action rows and outcomes).
- Relationship edges are simulated independently within each scenario family.

## Labels and targets
`overall_risk_label`, `liquidity_stress_label`, `relationship_risk_label`, and `growth_readiness_label` are generated by documented deterministic rule logic (`SIMULATOR_RULES_V1`). They are **not** historical default, bankruptcy, fraud, closure, or distress labels. Use them to test a prototype and controlled scenarios only.

## Split policy
Base business families are assigned to train/validation/test (70%/15%/15%). Every row from the same `scenario_family_id`, including snapshots and before/after mitigation variants, stays in the same partition. Fit all preprocessing on training data only.

## Suggested modelling sequence
1. Validate data ranges and schema.
2. Train a transparent rule baseline.
3. Train a tabular Random Forest/CatBoost baseline.
4. Build a NetworkX/PyTorch Geometric graph from entity and relationship tables and train a suitable GNN.
5. Compare models on the same split and targets.
6. Report results as synthetic-scenario benchmark results only.

## Real data candidate investigated
The World Bank Enterprise Survey 2025 for Sri Lanka has an official catalogue listing 607 establishments and 336 variables. Its scope is national and the catalogue routes microdata access to the Enterprise Surveys portal. This package does not include that raw microdata because a verified downloadable Western Province-only extract was not obtained here. Once downloaded legitimately, inspect the documented region variable and filter by verified region coding before using it. It is not a source of confirmed historical risk labels or a ready-made transaction graph.

Official source: https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/8264
DCS SME Survey page: https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Industry/StaticalInformation/SMESurvey2023
